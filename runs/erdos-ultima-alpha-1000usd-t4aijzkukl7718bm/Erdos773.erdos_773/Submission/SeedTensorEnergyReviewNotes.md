## Exact exploratory counts

Research/SeedTensorEnergy.cpp enumerates every unordered pair, including
repeated entries, in the base-81 language generated by the VERIFIED
27-root seed CubicChecksumExample.roots. It sorts the integer squared sums.
For a sum with r off-diagonal representations and d diagonal representations,
it adds r*(r-1)/2 four-root supports and d*r three-root supports. Here d<=1.
Two distinct off-diagonal presentations of one sum cannot share an endpoint.
Four distinct strictly ordered values admit at most one equal-pair-sum
partition, so this counts supports without an orientation multiplicity.

The program is an exploratory exact-integer computation, NOT a Lean theorem.
Its output is not used as a trusted proof certificate.
