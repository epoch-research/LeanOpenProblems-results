# The field-arithmetic version of the cubic norm checksum

This is an auxiliary construction obstruction, not a settlement of Erdős 773.

## Verified module

FieldNineChecksum.lean imports only FormalConjecturesUtil. It builds cleanly
and has a built olean. Its five principal axiom audits use only propext,
Classical.choice, and Quot.sound. Log: /tmp/field-nine-checksum.log.

The field is K=AdjoinRoot(X^2+1) over ZMod 3. The quadratic is proved
irreducible, and field_card proves Nat.card K=9. With theta the adjoined
root, theta^2=-1. The natural labels 0,...,8 encode the base-three
coordinates in the basis 1,theta.

The same cubic norm checksum is evaluated using K's FIELD operations,
not natural arithmetic modulo 9:

  F(a,b,c) = Norm(a+2+2c,c,b+c)+2a+2,
  Norm(x,y,z)=x^3+2x^2z-xy^2-3xyz+xz^2+y^3-yz^2+z^3.

The cubic X^3-X-1 remains irreducible over K. Indeed a root would satisfy
x^3=x+1, hence x^9=x+2, contradicting x^9=x in the nine-element field.
Thus the failure cannot be attributed to using ZMod 9 or to the cubic
becoming reducible.

## Exact collision

The checked digit tuples (a,b,c,d), with encode(d)=F(encode(a),...), are

  (6,6,0,3) ->2247,
  (0,5,0,1) ->774,
  (3,8,0,3) ->2262,
  (0,0,0,1) ->729,

where the integer root is a+9b+81c+729d. Their squares satisfy

  2247^2+774^2=2262^2+729^2=5648085.

The theorem squares_not_sidon proves failure of Sidonness for the entire
field-checksum digit family, defined by existential membership over the
four canonical digits. It includes all roots generated by this encoding.

## Scope and verification caution

The exploratory script Research/FieldNineChecksum.py uses explicit exact
F_9 arithmetic. Its first draft had a sign error in the norm expression;
Lean's attempted certificate exposed the error. The script was corrected,
and ONLY the new witness above is certified by the final Lean module.
No statement depends on the incorrect first-screen witness.

This concerns one field encoding and one fixed checksum formula at modulus
nine. No assertion about all bases, all field encodings, eventual failures,
or sizes of Sidon subsets follows. No original-conjecture exponent improved.
