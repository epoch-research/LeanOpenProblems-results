## Interfaces checked again

* JointMonotoneProfileOperatorExplore controls both profiles within ONE modulus.
  Its bounded height range still precedes the modulus.
* JointCoprimeThicknessExplore compares thicknesses for the SAME prime. It is
  not a cross-prime theorem.
* OuterMixedPrefixExplore retains a boundary-block error. Its estimate is not
  relative to the mass inside every short interval, and does not supply a fixed
  initial cutoff for the unbounded harmonic height profile.
* UniversalCompleteNaturalExplore and UniversalBernsteinActualExplore still
  require an integer-valued coarse convolution with the desired asymptotic.
* NaturalScaleRestorationProgress.md records a stronger finite restoration
  criterion than some older summaries: S(N)/sqrt(N log N) -> 0 suffices there.
  The constructed host's upper-excess demand is still not proved to satisfy it,
  and the required aggregate incidence estimate is also absent.
* BalancedRepairParametersExplore has an exact two-type designated-pair
  cancellation. Undesignated counts and infinite coarse feasibility remain
  separate requirements.
* SharpCapRoundingObstructionExplore uses a signed prefix mean tending to zero
  after division by N. The conjectured pointwise relative limit only gives an
  o(N log N) cumulative error, not that stronger signed-mean hypothesis.
* SharpFluctuationExplore already rules out any proposed exact digit-length
  formula r(n)=c log n+O(1) with c nonzero. It does not rule out o(log n) error.
* Growing-order Gaussian moment bounds cannot be invoked generically; the
  checked finite and natural-annular counterexamples remain relevant.
* The canonical harmonic floor rounding has a checked moving-window limit,
  not a pointwise limit. Its numerical holes and peaks are not an asymptotic
  disproof, and no persistence theorem was obtained.

## Informal investigations, not Lean results

Further consideration was given to cross-prime models, quadratic-extension
parameter slices, variable-width digit codes, complementary sign recursions,
coordinated signed edits, nonlinear rescaling, and profinite/rotation sampling.
None produced an unrestricted construction, uniform finite-prefix feasibility,
pointwise sublogarithmic quadratic rounding, or a universal contradiction.
No unproved estimate from those discussions should be treated as a lemma.

In particular, uniform counting asymptotics cannot be substituted for pointwise
endpoint distributions in a weighted-convolution argument. Entrywise-flat fine
kernels cannot silently be used at subunit mean. Replacing an increasing field
or a phase pattern does not preserve ordinary prefixes by any theorem reviewed.
